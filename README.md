Shows posts from every community your Lemmy account is subscribed to, in one timeline.

Enter your instance URL (for example https://lemmy.world), plus your username and password. The password is stored in this connector's variables on your device and is only sent to your own instance.

Actions on each post: Upvote, Downvote, Remove Vote, and Comment. Comment opens the composer and posts a top-level comment on the post.

Requires Tapestry 2.0 or later.
