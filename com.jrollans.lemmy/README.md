Shows posts from every community your Lemmy account is subscribed to, in one timeline.

Enter your instance URL (for example https://lemmy.world), plus your username and password. The password is stored in this connector's variables on your device and is only sent to your own instance.

Actions on posts and comments: Upvote, Downvote, Remove Vote, Bookmark (Lemmy's save), and Comment or Reply. Posts also have a Thread button that loads the comment tree.

Turn on "Include replies & private messages" to also bring in answers to your comments, comments on your posts, and private messages sent to you. Replies are marked with a note saying what they are replying to.

Requires Tapestry 2.0 or later.
